# AI/ML idea checks

Raise a point only if the idea text leaves it unanswered:
- Data: where does the input or training data come from, and who owns it?
- Need: is AI actually required, or would simple rules work?
- Success metric: improvement over what baseline, measured how?
- Failure cases: what happens when the output is wrong or made up, and who is affected?
- Privacy: is personal data sent to a third-party model?
- Cost and speed once many users arrive

Put each point under Assumptions or Clarification questions.
Quote the phrase that triggered it (for example "AI-powered").
