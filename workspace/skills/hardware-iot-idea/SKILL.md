# Hardware/IoT idea checks

Raise a point only if the idea text leaves it unanswered:
- Which components are named, and what does one unit cost?
- Power source and battery life
- Connectivity: what happens with no internet?
- Sensor accuracy and calibration
- Environment: dust, water, heat, vibration
- Can the idea be demoed without the hardware?
- Safety or regulatory concerns

Put each point under Assumptions or Clarification questions.
