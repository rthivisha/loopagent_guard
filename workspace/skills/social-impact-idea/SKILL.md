# Social impact idea checks

Raise a point only if the idea text leaves it unanswered:
- Who exactly benefits, and how do we know they have this problem?
- Who pays for it, and what happens after the demo or grant?
- What stops people from adopting it (literacy, language, trust, cost)?
- Is there a partner organisation on the ground?
- How is impact measured?

Put each point under Assumptions or Clarification questions.
